How it works

Two parts. One very good night.

  1. 1

    We film it

    We spend time with you and your people, in your space. Interviews, natural sound, the moments nobody planned. No stock smiles, no teleprompter. The story comes from the people telling it.

  2. 2

    We host the watch party

    Then we premiere it. We set up the screen and the room, and you invite the people who matter: customers, donors, family, and the neighbour who keeps asking what it is you actually do.

You keep the film. Your community keeps talking about the night.
